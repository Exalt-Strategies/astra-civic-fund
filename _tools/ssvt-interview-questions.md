# Sri Siva Vishnu Temple: interview questions for the committee

The published profile (stories/sri-siva-vishnu-temple.html) uses only SSVT's own
public pages. These questions are for a real conversation with board and committee
members, so their own words can be added as a Q&A section. Ask permission to quote
by name and to take photos.

## Engagement
1. What brings a new volunteer in, and what keeps them coming back after the first festival?
2. Which committee is the hardest to staff, and why?
3. How has volunteering changed between the founding generation and their children?
4. Aakaar, the youth and community service committee, has an open coordinator seat. What would you want a young person in that role to build?

## Impact
5. Beyond worship, what does the temple do for families that people outside the congregation would not see?
6. What has the Mitra Mandala (Circle of Friends) network looked like in practice?
7. How does the temple show up in the wider Lanham and Prince George's County community?
8. What do you want the temple to look like after the 2027 Jeernodharana Kumbhabhishekam, beyond the building itself?

## Governance
9. Why did the founders choose a trust, a board and an endowment rather than a looser structure?
10. What would you tell a young group starting a temple, association or nonprofit today about governance?

Keep the conversation non-political. SSVT is a 501(c)(3) and should not be asked
about candidates, parties or elections.
