What we help build

From a first idea to a filed, compliant organization.

Political infrastructure starts with paperwork most people never get taught. Each of these comes with its own rules, filings, and deadlines. We help you pick the right vehicle, then match you with someone who has done it before.

Nonprofit 501(c)(3)

Charitable, educational, and community organizations. Incorporation, bylaws, board setup, the IRS exemption application, and state charity registration.

Social welfare 501(c)(4)

Advocacy and issue campaigns that need room to lobby. Formation, the IRS notice and exemption filings, and the limits on political activity.

Political action committee

Federal or state PACs that support candidates. Registration, choosing a treasurer, contribution rules, and the reporting calendar.

Candidate committee

Running for school board, city council, the statehouse, or Congress. Committee registration, campaign finance compliance, and a filing plan.

Ballot and issue committees

Campaigns for or against a local or state ballot measure, with the registration and disclosure each state requires.

Lobbying and advocacy

Federal and state lobbying registration, advocacy days at the capitol, and how to meet with officials the right way.

Community and cultural associations

Temples, gurdwaras, cultural and professional associations that want stronger governance and a civic voice.

Campus organizations

Student groups that want to register voters, host candidates, or start a chapter, within university and state rules.

Fiscal sponsorship

Start your work under an established sponsor while you get ready to incorporate on your own.

How it works

Three steps, no cost to you.

The professionals in our network volunteer their time because they want more Indian Americans in civic life. You bring the project. We do the matching.

  1. Tell us what you are building.Use the form below. Share where you are, what you have done so far, and what is getting in the way.
  2. We match you with the right person.An attorney, accountant, lobbyist, or compliance specialist from our network, usually in your state.
  3. Get set up, then stay compliant.Formation and filings first, then the reporting, training, and introductions that keep the work going.

About you

Information on this page is general and is not legal, tax, or financial advice. Astra Civic Fund is a 501(c)(4) and does not itself provide legal services.

Astra Civic Fund is a 501(c)(4) social welfare organization building political power for the next generation of Indian Americans. We are bipartisan, Midwest-rooted, and organized around domestic American policy.
